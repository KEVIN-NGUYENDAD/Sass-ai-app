## 🚀 Quick Tips

**For maximum benefit:**
1. **Be specific** - "Configure OSPF on Cisco 2900" is better than "Tell me about OSPF"
2. **Ask step-by-step** - "Step 1: verify connectivity"
3. **Request examples** - "Give me a Cisco config example"
4. **Ask for diagrams** - "Draw the network topology"
5. **Follow up** - "Why would we use this approach?"

**Example conversation:**
```
Q: "What's VLAN?"
A: [Definition]

Q: "How to configure it on Cisco switch?"
A: [Step-by-step]

Q: "Can you give me an example config?"
A: [Code example]

Q: "How to route between VLANs?"
A: [Advanced topic]
```

---

**📌 Pro Tips:**
- Ask for "step-by-step" explanations
- Request "practical examples"
- Ask "why" and "when" not just "what"
- Request "diagram" for visual topics
- Ask for "best practice" recommendations
